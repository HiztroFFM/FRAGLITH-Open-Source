# FRAGLITH • Open Source

### Learn • Build • Share

**An open community for Counter-Strike 2 creators and developers.**

FRAGLITH brings people together to learn, build, and share knowledge about CS2 development. Whether you're just getting started or already making tools and content, you're welcome here.

[**Join our Discord community**](https://discord.gg/Aq8NgGswN)

---

## What we focus on

- **CS2 plugin development:** programming, debugging, and shared solutions
- **3D model porting:** workflows, tools, and troubleshooting
- **AG2 animations:** learning animation pipelines and model integration
- **Open-source resources:** documentation, examples, and collaborative projects
- **Community support:** developers helping developers

## Our approach

**Knowledge should be shared, not gatekept.**

We want to make CS2 development more accessible through clear explanations, open discussion, and responsible collaboration.

> This repository is the starting point for FRAGLITH. Project files, examples, and guides will be published as they become available.

## Get involved

1. Join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/Aq8NgGswN).
2. Introduce yourself and tell us what you're working on.
3. Ask questions, share your experience, or contribute documentation and code.
4. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting a contribution.
