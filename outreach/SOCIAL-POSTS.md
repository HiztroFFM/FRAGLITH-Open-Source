## Developer-focused post (Reddit or technical forums, only where allowed)

**Title:** We started an open CS2 development knowledge base for plugins, Source 2 models and AG2 troubleshooting

We've been compiling practical notes from CS2 server/plugin debugging into a public resource. It includes CounterStrikeSharp DLL troubleshooting, SwiftlyS2 migration considerations, Workshop/content delivery checks, ModelDoc and AG2/VData notes, and a small Python log-category utility.

The project is primarily documentation at this stage, not a drop-in plugin pack. Some animated weapon/AG2 problems are explicitly flagged as unresolved.

Feedback on inaccuracies and original contributions are welcome.

Repo: https://github.com/HiztroFFM/FRAGLITH-Open-Source
